Roadmap at a glance

Seven modules, each ending in a gate you must pass before moving on. Modules 1, 2 and 4 are independent, so you can alternate if one drags. Module 3 needs 1 and 2, Module 5 needs 3 and 4, Module 6 needs 5, and Module 7 needs everything.
