Module 3: Ego and consensus embeddings about 8 hours

Here you split each agent's observation in two and train the decentralized baseline that LatentToM must beat. The paper splits each arm's observation into an ego part and a consensus part, and gives each its own visual encoder.

What to learn

  • The ego input is the end-effector camera plus the arm's pose. The consensus input is the fixed third-person camera that sees the shared workspace.
  • The two encoders produce an ego embedding and a consensus embedding. The paper does not give their dimensions, so pick one (128 is a fine start) and keep it fixed.
  • Why split: ego information is private to one arm, while consensus information describes the shared scene, so the arms can be asked to agree on it.
  • Optional background: contrastive losses such as InfoNCE are another way to align two views, and a useful comparison for Module 5.
