Module 2: Imitation learning and your testbed about 15 hours

This module builds the testbed: a two-agent Push-T simulator, an expert that produces demonstrations, and a centralized diffusion policy (CDP) that serves as your upper bound. The paper's own CDP baseline plays the same role.

What to learn

  • Behavior cloning and compounding error. You know DAgger from RL, so treat this as the supervised view of the same problem.
  • Action chunking, as in ACT and ALOHA, and why it reduces compounding error.
  • Image encoders for policies: ResNet-18 with GroupNorm instead of BatchNorm, as Diffusion Policy does.
  • How the official repo stores demonstrations (a zarr replay buffer) and samples training windows.
